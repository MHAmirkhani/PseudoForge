ADPACK per-element input decks, from the ADPACK 2.2 distribution (work/).

These are the author's own parameters -- cut-off radii, reference
configurations, pseudization scheme -- not PseudoForge's.  They are bundled so
that generation works offline, in the same way fhi98PP's bin/Elements database
is bundled.

Upstream: http://www.openmx-square.org/adpack/adpack.html
Author:   Taisuke Ozaki

Unmodified.  The one deck that ADPACK itself declines (Te, whose
Troullier-Martins root-finder rejects the shipped radii) is left exactly as
distributed: adjusting those radii would be a physics change, and belongs in a
separate, clearly labelled patch rather than in the bundled data.
