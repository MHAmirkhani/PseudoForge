GIPAW pseudopotential input decks, from Davide Ceresoli's published repository.

  https://github.com/dceresoli/gipaw-pseudopotentials
  Directory: pseudos/<Z>-<El>/<name>/<El>.in

17 ld1.x decks: aluminium (PBE and PZ, Troullier-Martins) and the fifteen
lanthanides (PBE, ultrasoft with semicore states).  Every one sets
lgipaw_reconstruction, which is what makes the result usable for NMR and EPR.

Unmodified.  These are the author's parameters; PseudoForge runs them and
retargets only the functional and relativity the run asked for.
