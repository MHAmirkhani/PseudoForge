Marburg PAW data sets for the actinoids -- generation inputs
============================================================

These are the ld1.x input decks published by the authors, redistributed here
verbatim so PseudoForge can reproduce the data sets without a download.  They
are not PseudoForge's work and PseudoForge's licence does not apply.

  source : https://www.uni-marburg.de/de/fb15/arbeitsgruppen/ag-kraus/forschung/paw_datasets_for_the_actinoids
  terms  : see the distribution
  layout : scalar/ from scalar-relativistic-basis.zip
           full/   from fully-relativistic-basis.zip

Nothing here has been modified.  They are ordinary ld1.x decks, so PseudoForge
generates them with the same engine it uses for PSlibrary.
